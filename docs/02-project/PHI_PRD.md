## Purpose

Define the product requirements for Project PHI, a research-grade quantitative market intelligence platform. This document exists to align stakeholders on vision, scope, and requirements ahead of the next architecture phase.

## Scope

Covers product-level requirements only: vision, users, goals, and requirements. Does not cover system architecture (see [../03-architecture/README.md](../03-architecture/README.md)) or AI operating system role behavior (see [../01-ai-playbooks/PHI_SYSTEM.md](../01-ai-playbooks/PHI_SYSTEM.md)).
