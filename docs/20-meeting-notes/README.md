# 20 · Meeting Notes

**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

## Purpose

Archive meeting notes and summaries relevant to Project PHI.

## Scope

Covers meeting records only. Decisions that come out of a meeting should also be captured in 18-decisions.

## What Belongs Here

- Meeting notes and summaries
- Action items and owners

## Naming Convention

Use a date prefix plus kebab-case, e.g. `2026-08-02-standup.md`.

## Example Files

- `2026-08-02-standup.md`
- `2026-08-02-planning-review.md`

## Best Practices

- Use the MEETING_TEMPLATE.md in 15-templates for consistency
- Capture action items with clear owners and due dates
- Link out to 18-decisions for any decisions made
