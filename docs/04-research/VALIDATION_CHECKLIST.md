# PHI Validation Checklist

Before accepting a research result:

- [ ] Data is point-in-time correct.
- [ ] Train and test periods are separated.
- [ ] No future information leaks into features.
- [ ] Baselines are defined before evaluation.
- [ ] Randomized/placebo controls are included where applicable.
- [ ] Transaction costs and realistic execution assumptions are documented.
- [ ] Walk-forward or equivalent out-of-sample validation is performed.
- [ ] Multiple-testing risk is considered.
- [ ] Results are reproducible from recorded inputs and configuration.
- [ ] Negative results are preserved and reported.
