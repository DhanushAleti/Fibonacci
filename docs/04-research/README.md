# 04 · Research

**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

## Purpose

Hold research notes, findings, and exploratory analysis that inform product and technical direction.

## Scope

Covers open-ended investigation — literature review, competitive research, exploratory prototypes. Does not cover finalized specs or decisions.

## What Belongs Here

- Research briefs and findings
- Literature/competitive reviews
- Exploratory analysis notes

## Naming Convention

Use kebab-case markdown files with a date prefix where useful, e.g. `2026-08-02-topic.md`.

## Example Files

- `research-template-usage.md`
- `2026-08-02-model-comparison.md`

## Best Practices

- Date-stamp research notes so freshness is obvious
- Summarize key findings at the top of each doc
- Promote validated findings into 18-decisions or 03-architecture
